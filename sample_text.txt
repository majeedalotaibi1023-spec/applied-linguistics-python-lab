I enjoy learning English because I can communicate with more people. Speaking is sometimes difficult because I need more time to think about the words I want to use.

I like learning new vocabulary, but remembering new words can be difficult. Using the words in conversation helps me remember them better.

Listening is challenging when people speak quickly. I understand more when I can listen to something more than once.

I feel more confident when I practice English with other learners. Group activities give me opportunities to speak and learn from other people.

Writing helps me think carefully about grammar and vocabulary. Sometimes I know what I want to say, but I am not sure how to express the idea clearly.

Technology helps me practice English outside the classroom. I use videos and language learning tools when I want additional practice.

Speaking regularly makes me more confident. I still make mistakes, but I feel that mistakes are a normal part of learning a language.

I learn vocabulary better when new words appear in meaningful situations. It is more difficult for me to remember words when I study them only as a list.
